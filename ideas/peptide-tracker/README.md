# Peptide / GLP-1 tracker idea

Research snapshot: **14 October 2026**. Initial market: United States. Proposed first release: English-language iPhone app. These are planning assumptions, not a released product or a user-approved implementation stack.

## Recommendation

Proceed with a small validation effort and MVP. The strongest proposed entry point is people managing several user-entered schedules and containers who find existing trackers difficult to operate. The proposed promise is **“Know what you recorded, when, and how much is left.”**

Compete through a coherent workflow: record actual use, update the correct container, correct a mistake without breaking inventory, and retain/export the history. A generic calculator, PDF, chatbot, or another GLP-1 dashboard is insufficient differentiation.

The earlier assessment was 7/10 for a focused product and 4/10 for a simple clone. These are subjective judgments, not probabilities. The user requested a binary recommendation; the answer was **build**, starting small and validating payment rather than committing a large budget.

## Status and boundaries

This is documentation of the discussion and research, not executable app code. Public feature descriptions were not tested in installed apps. Reviews describe individual experiences; they do not prove current defects or population-wide dissatisfaction. Revenue/download figures on screenshots are unverified estimates. Feature, price and policy observations are historical to the research date and should be rechecked before launch.

The documents are primarily English. Original detailed analyses remain in Polish to preserve the evidence and qualifications developed in the conversation. The raw exports include public review handles and developer replies. No user authentication credentials are included.
