# Decision history and unresolved questions

## Evolution of the proposal

1. **Initial screen-based assessment:** promising specific keywords and apparent revenue leaders justified investigation, not a revenue forecast. Subjective assessment favored a focused build over a clone.
2. **Broad independent research:** separated GLP-1 from other peptides, identified free/general-health competition, and raised calculator/data constraints. An initial hypothesis emphasized a private treatment diary and clinician report.
3. **Four-competitor review research:** established that PDF/provider channels and many basic functions already exist. Shifted the proposed entry point toward people with multiple schedules and supply complexity, with easy correction and portable history.
4. **Concrete MVP proposal:** four tabs, short first use, actual-use records, linked supply, symptoms/weight, backups/export and clear Free/Pro. Calculator became an optional gated module. iPhone/English/US and trial/prices are assumptions/proposals.
5. **Documentation request:** user authorized consolidating provided and found information and uploading it to Jaqwilk/APPIDEAS. This does not authorize implementation, deployment, contacting users or sending messages to third parties.

## Proposed decisions

- Build a small testable product rather than reject solely because of competition.
- Keep actual records separate from future plans.
- Favor a coherent diary/supply workflow over breadth.
- Preserve data/export after subscription expiry.
- Stable adult interface with optional extra modules.
- Four primary tabs: Today, History, Supply, Progress.
- No personalized dosing/treatment recommendations.

## Not yet decided or validated

- Product name, brand, final platform scope and technology stack.
- Representative size of the complex peptide-tracking segment.
- Whether target users will switch and what import formats are available.
- Price, trial and exact Free/Pro limits.
- Calculator qualification, store acceptance and applicable jurisdiction-specific obligations.
- Backup architecture, encryption, cross-device recovery and later cross-platform sync.
- Offline conflict resolution, time-zone policy and detailed scheduling semantics.
- Exact initial item list and terminology; no inference that an item is approved or safe for human use.
- Medical/clinical review scope for any educational content.
- Team, development budget, effort, launch date and acquisition budget.

## Claims that must not become marketing facts

Screenshot revenue is not verified developer earnings. Review averages are not population satisfaction. Declared download counts are not active/paying users. An estimated medication curve is not a blood measurement. A feature request is not proof of clinical feasibility. Our proposed workflow is not established market exclusivity.
