# Reseller inventory and profit organizer

Status: **weaker conditional hypothesis**, not a strong ASO opportunity established by the screenshots. Snapshot: 14 October 2026.

## Problem and audience

People buying goods for resale need item-level purchase cost, photos, physical storage location, listing state, sale proceeds and costs. Proposed initial focus: one category such as second-hand clothes rather than all goods and marketplaces.

Working workflow: buy -> photograph -> label storage -> list -> find the sold item -> record costs/proceeds. The organizing value is clearer than a generic “reselling app,” but payment demand remains unmeasured.

## Unmeasured terms to check first

reseller inventory; reseller tracker; inventory tracker; flip tracker; profit tracker. No popularity/difficulty values were supplied for these exact phrases. Do not infer their demand from reselling=21.

## Small MVP hypothesis

1. Quick item/photo/cost entry and optional category.
2. Box/rack location and searchable item ID.
3. Bought/listed/sold states, listing reference and date.
4. User-entered sale price, fees, shipping and other costs with explainable arithmetic.
5. Item history and export.

No marketplace automation, cross-listing integrations, payments or tax advice at launch. Do not claim automatic listings or fee accuracy across platforms. A focused storage workflow could differentiate only if actual sellers find it materially easier.

## Why retain it

The user is operating a revenue-generating activity; time and item-location mistakes may have visible cost. This is a reason to interview them, not proof they will subscribe.

## Existing competition

[Sourcerer](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sourcerer-reseller-inventory/id6780601247) advertises resale inventory and item costs/profit along with broader sourcing functions. Its existence establishes competing scope. The listing observed during the idea round had very few ratings, so it is not evidence of a large validated market or financial success.

## Next decision

Check the organizer-specific keyword set and interview several active sellers. Advance only if they have a repeated problem with current notes/spreadsheets/tools and show interest in paying for the exact workflow. Otherwise deprioritize instead of continually searching broad synonyms.
