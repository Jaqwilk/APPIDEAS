# Shift calendar and pay tracker

Status: **strongest new alternative to investigate**, not a validated replacement for peptide. Snapshot: 14 October 2026. Suggested first market: US/iPhone/English, subject to confirmation.

## Problem and audience

People with rotating shifts, more than one job, variable hourly rates or premiums need to plan work, record actual hours and check their own expected pay. Possible audiences include nurses, warehouse workers, security staff and first responders; these are proposed segments, not validated buyer groups.

Working promise: **“Add your schedule, record actual hours, and compare your estimate with the pay you received.”**

## Proposed differentiator

Fast schedule entry, potentially from a photo with explicit review/correction, followed by transparent comparison of planned vs actual hours and expected vs received pay. Schedule-plus-pay already exists; the opportunity would be execution for a chosen group's workflow, not inventing the category.

## Small MVP hypothesis

1. Calendar with shifts, reusable patterns and easy actual-hour corrections.
2. Multiple user-entered jobs/rates and explicit break/premium assumptions.
3. Estimated earnings using the user's configured rules, with an explainable breakdown.
4. Received-pay entries and discrepancy view; no claim of legal payroll accuracy.
5. Reminders, numerical history and export.

Photo import is a prototype candidate, not a requirement to build before validating routine use. Recognized dates/times must be confirmed before creating shifts. Taxes, state-specific payroll advice, employer integrations and automated disputes are outside initial scope.

## Why it may work

The task recurs and the benefit is demonstrable. This is a product judgment, not measured retention. It avoids peptide-related medical complexity, but wage-rule correctness, time zones, overnight shifts and daylight saving remain material challenges.

## Main uncertainty

“Paycheck calculator” may mean net pay after deductions rather than shift earnings. Inspect ranked apps and reviews before treating its popularity as demand for this product. No competitor revenue, written-review corpus or paid conversion was collected.

## Competitor example and source

[Shift Calendar & Paycheck](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shift-calendar-paycheck/id6769425560) advertises shift planning, overtime and earnings. It confirms existing functional competition, not a gap. This public listing was reviewed during the idea round; no hands-on testing was performed.
