## Why these were retained

- Shift/pay: a recurring organizing task and comparatively interesting observed keyword combinations.
- Receipts: an observed scanner signal warrants further investigation, although it does not validate the originally proposed warranty use case.
- Resellers: direct operational value for people reselling goods, but it remains a conditional hypothesis rather than a stronger ASO opportunity.

## Directions not retained as recommended candidates

The earlier general estimate/quotation app did not show a better ASO opportunity than peptide in the supplied results; “quote maker” was also ambiguous with image/citation creation. Interview/public-speaking terms were weak or difficult in the supplied screens. Pantry/fridge results did not yet establish attractive demand for the proposed inventory workflow. Warranty tracking is not independently validated by receipt-scanning popularity.

This is a selection judgment for the current App Store-led exploration, not a claim these excluded businesses cannot work. Their raw screenshots and full keyword datasets are intentionally not uploaded here, consistent with the request to retain only promising alternatives.

## Change in recommendation

The conversation initially explored a narrowly focused B2B quoting tool as potentially attractive economically. Subsequent screenshots favored peptide for ASO. A second idea round proposed shift tracking, reselling, interview practice, pantry and warranties. The latest evidence promotes shift tracking to the top alternative; receipt and reseller directions are retained only with the qualifications above. No final switch away from peptide has been approved.

## Definitions and verification

[Astro keyword definitions](https://tryastro.app/docs/data-overview-keywords/) explain the source's score meanings. Relevant competitor pages establish advertised scope only; none were installed or tested in this follow-up. Existing products are category examples, not proof of a market gap or financial success.

Before choosing a new build, collect the first 5–10 actual apps for relevant keywords, their exact identities, prices/free limits, estimates with scope/period, and written reviews. Compare user tasks and measure payment rather than choosing only the highest popularity number.
